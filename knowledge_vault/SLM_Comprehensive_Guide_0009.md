# 3. Model Selection Guide: When to Use Which SLM

Meta Llama Series: Choose Meta Llama (3.2 1B/3B) when you require broad out-of-the-box framework integration (LangChain, LlamaIndex, vLLM, Ollama) and a well-tested community ecosystem. SmolLM2 Series: Choose SmolLM2 when legal and compliance teams require 100% transparency into the training corpus and data provenance.
