# 1. SLM vs. LLM: Definitive Comparison & Architectural Differences

While Large Language Models (LLMs, typically > 70B parameters) are designed as general-purpose cognitive engines optimized for broad creative writing, open-ended brainstorming, and cross-domain reasoning, Small Language Models (SLMs, typically < 10B parameters) are engineered for high-density, bounded enterprise execution. They achieve near-frontier performance on specific workloads while maintaining a footprint small enough to run on single GPUs, edge devices, or local client workstations. Evaluation Dimension: Parameter Scale; Large Language Models (LLMs): 70 Billion to 1+ Trillion parameters; Small Language Models (SLMs): 0.5 Billion to 10 Billion parameters Evaluation Dimension: Hardware Footprint; Large Language Models (LLMs): Requires multi-GPU clusters (H100/A100 array); Small Language Models (SLMs): Runs on single GPU (T4/L4/RTX 4090) or CPU edge
