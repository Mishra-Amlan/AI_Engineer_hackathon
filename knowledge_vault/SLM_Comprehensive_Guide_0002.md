# 1. SLM vs. LLM: Definitive Comparison & Architectural Differences

Evaluation Dimension: Inference Latency (TTFT); Large Language Models (LLMs): Higher latency due to memory bandwidth constraints; Small Language Models (SLMs): Ultra-low latency (< 50 ms time-to-first-token) Evaluation Dimension: Hosting Economics; Large Language Models (LLMs): High cloud API cost or expensive dedicated nodes; Small Language Models (SLMs): Extremely low cost; highly viable for local/on-premise deployment Evaluation Dimension: Customization & Tuning; Large Language Models (LLMs): Cost-prohibitive full fine-tuning; reliant on prompt engineering or API LoRA; Small Language Models (SLMs): Fast, efficient QLoRA fine-tuning on consumer hardware Evaluation Dimension: Domain Specificity; Large Language Models (LLMs): Broad generalist capabilities across diverse arts/sciences; Small Language Models (SLMs): Highly tunable specialist for code, logs, compliance, and routing Architectural Innovations in SLMs
